# Decisions

Register of the Agentic Build Graph. Decisions made during increments, so they are not re-litigated
next session. One entry each: date, the decision, who made it (user decisions are the user's — G1/G4),
and the reason. Keep the reason; a decision without its reason gets reversed by the next reader.
